# CodePlayground — Typing Race

> **Type fast. Type right. Blame the keyboard.**

A simple typing race for the team.

Pick a snippet, start typing, and try to beat everyone's score.

### The Rules

* Type what's on the screen.
* Don't copy-paste. We know what you did.
* Speed gets you points.
* Accuracy saves your reputation.
* Mistakes are between you and your keyboard.

### The Goal

Get the highest **WPM**, maintain good accuracy, and take the top spot on the leaderboard.

> **Fast fingers. Clean code. Questionable confidence.**

### Game Flow

```text
Pick a challenge
      ↓
Start typing
      ↓
Try not to panic
      ↓
Finish
      ↓
Check your score
      ↓
Challenge someone else
```

> **If you lose, it was probably your keyboard.**
